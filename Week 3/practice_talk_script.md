# Week 3 Practice Talk Script
**"One insight, no jargon" · ~3 minutes**

---

## Slide 1 — Title (~15 sec)

- I'm Oussama. I'm building a model to predict U.S. bank undercapitalization. This week, I went through the data looking for early signs of undercapitalization.

---

## Slide 2 — Undercapitalization (~15 sec)

- Quick context so my target is clear to everyone.
- Every bank keeps a reserve of money, so there's always enough for people to withdraw.
- By law, that reserve has to stay above 8 percent, or the bank falls to undercapitalized status, which puts it at risk of failing. That the risk is what im trying to predict before it happens.

---

## Slide 3 — The finding (~55 sec)

- Here are my findings, this chart shows six indicators trends for both healthy banks in yellow, and undercapitalized banks in blue
-  undercapitalized banks keep worsening across all six indicators for two years before they are finally flagged, while healthy banks stay relatively stable.
- The difference shows that this effect is not due to the economy, it is bank specific.
- It also shows warning signs starts appearing on average up to 8 quarters early. which means that we can predict the distress up to two years early
