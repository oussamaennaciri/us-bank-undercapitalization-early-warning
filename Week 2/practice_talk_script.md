# Practice Talk Script — "My Data & Why It Fits"

**Slide 1 — Title**
- Morning. I'm Oussama, and I'm building an early-warning model for U.S. bank capital distress.
- Simple question: which banks are headed for trouble, and how much runway do they have.

**Slide 2 — The Data**
- Here's what I'm working with. FDIC bank filings going back to 1984, straight through to today.
- That's 1.68 million bank-quarter records. Real numbers, not a sample.
- On top of that, macro context, rates, unemployment, the stuff that moves the whole system.

**Slide 3 — Fit**
- Why does this data work? Two things.
- It's reliable, it comes straight from FDIC and FRED, the same places regulators pull from.
- And it's fitting, it has the exact ratios regulators use to call a bank undercapitalized. Not a proxy. The real thing.

**Slide 4 — Limitations**
- Now, I'm not going to stand here and tell you it's perfect.
- It's quarterly, so it lags. Won't catch a 48-hour run like SVB.
- What I'm predicting is rare, so the data's imbalanced, and it's self-reported, so there's bias risk built in.
- Doesn't change the call. Right data for the question. We keep it, and we build around the limits.
